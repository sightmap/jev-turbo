Without a map, the loop now finds the entries of a repeated list on its own: a container whose parent holds siblings of the same shape and whose subtree carries a title (a heading, else the longest link). Candidates inside an entry are offered as a group headed by that title, a promoted control says which entry it is in, and the history line does too, so sixteen add buttons with one name are told apart by their card the way a mapped ProductCard tells them apart. On the IKEA variants suite without a map: 15 of 15 goals reached from 10, wasted steps 15 from 34, unsure picks 38 from 79.
