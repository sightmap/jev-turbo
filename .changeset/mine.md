`jev-turbo mine RESULT.json ...` reads run files and prints the step sequences that recur across their successful runs as a sightkick tools.yaml draft: a sequence stays on one view, a navigating step can only end it after fills (a form and its submit) and otherwise stands alone, a fill's value key becomes a parameter, and the view a step reaches becomes a wait. On thirty saucedemo runs it drafts login, add_to_cart, open_cart, menu, checkout, the checkout form, finish and logout; on the IKEA runs, search, add_to_cart and open_bag, every journey those runs took.
